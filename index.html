About

I'm driven by curiosity and a love of storytelling. Everything I create is meant to inspire wonder and invite people into imaginative spaces where they can feel something new.

Whether through visual art, performance, direction, or writing, I'm always exploring how to create immersive experiences that transport and transform. My creative work is grounded in a belief that imagination is powerful, and that well-crafted worlds can change how people see themselves and the world around them.

I draw inspiration from fantasy, science fiction, nature, and the elemental forces that shape our world. My hope is that my work opens doors to new perspectives and invites audiences to become part of the worlds I create.
